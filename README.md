# Pixavelon Support

Pixavelon is a medieval house-politics role-playing game for iPhone: lead your noble house through intrigue, marriages, alliances, and war, season by season.

## Get help or report a problem

[Open an issue](https://github.com/clearfireblue/PixavelonSupport/issues/new) describing what happened. It helps to include:

- your iPhone model and iOS version
- the app version (shown in the App Store listing)
- what you were doing when the problem happened

## Common questions

**Where are my saves?**
Saves are kept on your iPhone, inside the app. Use the **Saves** button at the top of the game to switch between, rename, or delete saved houses.

**Will I lose my saves if I update the app?**
No. Updates keep your saves. Deleting the app deletes them.

**Does the game need the internet?**
No. Pixavelon plays fully offline.

## Privacy

See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md). In short: Pixavelon collects no data, and your saves never leave your device.
