# Pixavelon Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 25, 2026*

Pixavelon does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- **No data leaves your device.** Your saved games are kept only on your iPhone, inside the app. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else.
- **No accounts.** You don't sign in or create an account to play.
- **No tracking, analytics, or ads.** The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.
- **No internet connection needed.** The game runs entirely on your device.

Deleting the app deletes your saved games along with it.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the [Pixavelon support page](https://github.com/clearfireblue/PixavelonSupport).
